PACIFICON APP — RELEASE NOTES
=============================

A running log of what shipped, kept here so it travels with the data the app
reads. Newest first. Each entry holds the text submitted to the stores and the
longer version given to testers.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

        iOS build 202609181525  ·  Android versionCode 29829082
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

APP STORE — "What's New in This Version"

FOR TESTERS

  New
  • My Raffle Tickets (Information Hub). Enter the numbers on your stubs — one
    at a time, or a range like 100200-100299 for a book of tickets. The app
    compares them against every draw. Numbers you hold appear green on the Prize
    Draws page marked YOURS, and a notification arrives when one wins. Your
    numbers stay on the phone; nothing is uploaded.
  • Grand prizes. Each draw shows its prize, and the winner's name and callsign
    once drawn. The conference's Main Grand Prize has its own card at the top,
    with when it is drawn.
  • Hours page: registration & will call, cash sales, vendor exhibit hours.
  • Favourite vendors: star them like sessions, then filter by the star.
  • Overflow car park: address, hours, a Maps button, and now the turns written
    out for anyone not using a map.
  • Vendor Exhibit Hours head each day on the Schedule, as red cards.

  Fixed
  • Tapping below a card's star — near the map icon, especially at large text
    sizes — no longer favourites the session by mistake.
  • Winning numbers are shown in ascending order, however they were entered.
  • Clearing favourites in Settings now also clears the star filter.
  • Settings counts favourite vendors as well as sessions; with only vendors
    starred, Reset was greyed out.
  • "All" stays selected beside the star on the Schedule filters.
  • Android: text no longer clips with large or bold system fonts; the keyboard
    no longer covers the feedback box; the map leaves room for the list in
    landscape.

  Also
  • "Nonprofits" spelled consistently, and Pacificon carries its service mark
    throughout the app.
  • Content: banquet tickets must be collected from Registration by 4pm
    Saturday; new speaker photo (WA6HHQ) and biography (KJ6OUI); Prize Booth
    and organiser details.

  Please check
  1. My Raffle Tickets — add a single number and a range, check the total, and
     that "Remove all" asks first.
  2. Prize Draws — Draw 1 has published numbers. Add one of them as your ticket
     and it should turn green, marked YOURS.
  3. Star a card, then tap just below the star and beside the map icon. Neither
     should favourite anything. Worth trying with a large text size set in your
     phone's accessibility settings.
  4. Star a vendor but no sessions, then check Settings reads "1 vendor
     favorited" with Reset enabled.

  Note for editors
  From this version the app also fetches venue.json, so the whole Directions
  page — venue address, driving directions, parking notes, overflow car park —
  can be changed from this repository without a new release.
