PACIFICON APP — RELEASE NOTES
=============================

A running log of what shipped, kept here so it travels with the data the app
reads. Newest first. Each entry holds the text submitted to the stores and the
longer version given to testers.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.0.6  —  1 October 2026
        iOS build 202610011751  ·  Android versionCode 29847951
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

APP STORE — "What's New in This Version"

  • Add several ticket numbers at once: type them separated by commas, mixing
    single numbers and ranges
  • Tapping a win notification now opens My Tickets
  • A range typed backwards is no longer accepted silently
  • The page is now called My Tickets


GOOGLE PLAY — "What's new" (500 character limit)

  Enter several ticket numbers at once, separated by commas — single numbers and
  ranges together, for example 100234, 100300-100310, 100999.

  Tapping a "You won" notification now opens My Tickets.

  A range typed backwards, like 100234-987, is refused instead of being read
  the wrong way round.


FOR TESTERS

  • My Tickets takes a list. "100234, 100300-100310, 100999" becomes three
    entries totalling 13 tickets. Newlines and semicolons split too, so pasting
    from a message works.
  • Nothing is added unless every part reads. A bad part names itself — "abc is
    not a number or a range" — rather than quietly adding the rest, which would
    leave you believing you hold a ticket you do not.
  • A reversed range is refused. "100234-987" used to be read as 987-100234 and
    silently claimed 99,248 tickets.
  • Tapping a win notification opens My Tickets, whether the app was running or
    not.
  • The page is My Tickets, and its first line says "ticket numbers".

  Please check
  1. Paste or type a mixed list and confirm the total at the bottom.
  2. Try a list with one bad entry — nothing should be added.
  3. If a draw has numbers published, add one, close the app fully, and tap the
     notification when it arrives.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.0.5  —  18 September 2026
        iOS build 202609181525  ·  Android versionCode 29829082
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

APP STORE — "What's New in This Version"

  • My Tickets: enter your ticket numbers, or a whole range, and the app
    checks them against each prize draw for you
  • Grand prizes and winners now shown for every draw, with the Main Grand Prize
    at the top
  • New Hours page: registration, cash sales and vendor exhibit hours
  • Star your favourite vendors and filter by them
  • Step-by-step driving directions to the overflow car park
  • Vendor exhibit hours now head each day on the Schedule
  • Fixes: tapping near the map icon no longer favourites a session, winning
    numbers are listed in order, and layouts hold at large text sizes


GOOGLE PLAY — "What's new" (500 character limit)

  Prize draw tickets: enter your numbers, or a whole range, and the app checks
  them against every draw.

  Also new: grand prizes and winners on each draw, an Hours page (registration,
  cash sales, exhibit hall), favourite vendors, and directions to the overflow
  car park.

  Fixed: tapping near the map icon no longer favourites a talk, winning numbers
  are listed in order, and text no longer clips with large or bold system fonts.


FOR TESTERS

  New
  • My Tickets (Information Hub). Enter the numbers on your stubs — one
    at a time, or a range like 100200-100299 for a book of tickets. The app
    compares them against every draw. Numbers you hold appear green on the Prize
    Draws page marked YOURS, and a notification arrives when one wins. Your
    numbers stay on the phone; nothing is uploaded.
  • Grand prizes. Each draw shows its prize, and the winner's name and callsign
    once drawn. The conference's Main Grand Prize has its own card at the top,
    with when it is drawn.
  • Hours page: registration & will call, cash sales, vendor exhibit hours.
  • Favourite vendors: star them like sessions, then filter by the star.
  • Overflow car park: address, hours, a Maps button, and now the turns written
    out for anyone not using a map.
  • Vendor Exhibit Hours head each day on the Schedule, as red cards.

  Fixed
  • Tapping below a card's star — near the map icon, especially at large text
    sizes — no longer favourites the session by mistake.
  • Winning numbers are shown in ascending order, however they were entered.
  • Clearing favourites in Settings now also clears the star filter.
  • Settings counts favourite vendors as well as sessions; with only vendors
    starred, Reset was greyed out.
  • "All" stays selected beside the star on the Schedule filters.
  • Android: text no longer clips with large or bold system fonts; the keyboard
    no longer covers the feedback box; the map leaves room for the list in
    landscape.

  Also
  • "Nonprofits" spelled consistently, and Pacificon carries its service mark
    throughout the app.
  • Content: banquet tickets must be collected from Registration by 4pm
    Saturday; new speaker photo (WA6HHQ) and biography (KJ6OUI); Prize Booth
    and organiser details.

  Please check
  1. My Tickets — add a single number and a range, check the total, and
     that "Remove all" asks first.
  2. Prize Draws — Draw 1 has published numbers. Add one of them as your ticket
     and it should turn green, marked YOURS.
  3. Star a card, then tap just below the star and beside the map icon. Neither
     should favourite anything. Worth trying with a large text size set in your
     phone's accessibility settings.
  4. Star a vendor but no sessions, then check Settings reads "1 vendor
     favorited" with Reset enabled.

  Note for editors
  From this version the app also fetches venue.json, so the whole Directions
  page — venue address, driving directions, parking notes, overflow car park —
  can be changed from this repository without a new release.
